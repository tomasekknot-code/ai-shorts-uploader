AI Shorts Uploader

A personal automation tool for one YouTube channel.

AI Shorts Uploader is a private script that runs on its developer's own computer. It prepares one short educational video per day and uploads it to a single YouTube channel owned by the developer.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the developer's own YouTube channel and sets its scheduled publication time.
  • Reads back the uploaded video's status to confirm the upload succeeded.
  • Once a week, reads view and subscriber statistics for the developer's own videos.

Who can use it

Only the developer. There is no sign-up, no hosted service and no second user. The application is not distributed and is not offered to the public.
